Frequently Asked Questions

Is this video to text converter free?

Yes. The tool is designed to provide free transcription directly in your browser.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

The transcription pipeline runs locally in your browser. Your selected video is converted and processed on your device.

What languages are supported?

Whisper supports multilingual speech recognition. This tool provides common language selections such as English, Arabic, French, Spanish and German.

Can I convert MP4 videos?

Yes. FFmpeg.wasm is used in the browser to extract the audio track from supported video containers before sending the audio waveform to Whisper.
