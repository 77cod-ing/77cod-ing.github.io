How it works

  1. Choose a video

    Upload a file from your device, or paste a direct link to a hosted video/audio file.

  2. Audio is extracted locally

    The Web Audio API decodes the video and pulls out its audio track — inside your browser, in memory.

  3. On-device speech recognition

    A compact speech-to-text model (Whisper) runs in your browser via WebAssembly to transcribe the audio.

  4. Copy or download

    Review the transcript, copy it to your clipboard, or download it as a plain .txt file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool upload my video to a server?

No. The video never leaves your device. Both audio extraction and transcription run locally in your browser.

Can I convert a YouTube or Facebook video link to text?

Not directly — browsers can't fetch video streams from those platforms, and doing so would conflict with their terms of service. Download the video yourself and use the Upload tab instead.

Is this really free?

Yes, with no sign-up. The only one-time cost is downloading the speech-recognition model to your browser, which is then cached.

Which languages are supported?

Auto-detect, plus manual selection for English, Arabic, French, Spanish, and German.
