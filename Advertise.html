FAQ

Common Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, bank transfer. For enterprise orders, we can issue invoices with NET-30 terms.

What banner sizes do you support?

We support 728×90 (in-article), 1200×120 (top banner), 300×250 (sidebar), and custom sizes for featured tools and sponsored sections. If you need a different size, contact us and we'll accommodate.

What is your refund policy?

If we fail to deliver your placement as described within the agreed timeline, you're eligible for a full refund. We don't offer refunds based on performance metrics since we don't guarantee clicks — only placement and impressions.

Can I update my banner after it goes live?

Yes. You can update your banner creative once per billing period at no extra cost. Additional changes may incur a small fee. Just send the new design and we'll replace it within 24 hours.

Can I sponsor a specific article?

Yes. You can choose which articles your ad appears in, subject to availability. We can also recommend articles based on your target audience and the topics most relevant to your product.

Will my ad appear on mobile devices?

Yes. In-article banners and featured tools are fully responsive and visible on all devices. Sidebar cards are desktop-only by design, but we offer mobile-specific alternatives.

How long does my advertisement stay live?

Standard plans are monthly with auto-renewal. You can also opt for 3-month or 12-month plans at discounted rates. Since our articles are evergreen, your ad keeps generating value throughout.

Which countries will my ad be visible in?

Your ad will be visible worldwide. Our top traffic comes from USA, India, UK, Germany, and 140+ other countries. If you want to geo-target specific regions, contact us to discuss options.

Do you provide performance reports?

Yes. Starter plans get monthly impression reports. Professional and Enterprise plans get detailed reports including impressions, estimated clicks, and placement performance. We use privacy-friendly analytics.

Can I advertise my AI tool or SaaS product?

Absolutely. AI tools, SaaS products, hosting services, developer APIs, UI libraries, and programming courses are our most popular advertising categories. As long as your product is relevant to developers, it's a great fit.

Can you provide an invoice for my company?

Yes. We provide professional invoices with all required details (company name, address, tax ID, etc.) for accounting and expense purposes. Invoices are sent upon payment.

What kind of support do you offer?

All plans include email support with response within 24 hours. Enterprise plans include a dedicated account manager with priority support via email and scheduled calls. We help with banner design, placement strategy, and optimization.
